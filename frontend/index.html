INPUT 01What to bring

RequiredLoan dump: one row per loan/case with loan_id or account_no, the outcome to predict, product, amount, tenure, rate, EMI, LTV, disbursal date, repayment history (DPD, overdue, months on book) and non-PII applicant profile (age band, income, occupation, pincode-level geography).
RecommendedBureau: score, enquiries (3/6/12 m), live and closed trade lines, exposure, utilisation, worst DPD, write-offs — as a flat CSV or the raw bureau JSON.
RecommendedBank statement: average / minimum / month-end balances, credit and debit counts, salary regularity, bounces, existing EMI outflow. Monthly rows are fine.

INPUT 02Rules of the road

  • One common identifier across files. Names can differ (loan_id, ref_no, account_number); the engine finds the match and shows you the match rate.
  • Formats: CSV, bureau JSON / JSON lines, Parquet. Up to 20 GB per file; uploads are chunked and resume if the connection drops.
  • Outcome column: a 0/1 flag, yes/no, a class label, or a number you want thresholded (e.g. DPD > 90). You confirm the positive class.
  • Time column (disbursal date, snapshot month) unlocks true out-of-time validation.
  • At least 100 rows and 2 columns; more history means a stronger, more stable score.

INPUT 03Keep out & terms

  • Do not upload PII: customer name, mobile, email, address, PAN, Aadhaar, date of birth, or any account number other than the loan identifier. Anything that slips through is flagged and dropped before training.
  • Privacy: your data is used only to build the models and is deleted the moment results are delivered. Only aggregate metrics and the trained model bundle remain.
  • Pricing: free for now; after the introductory period ₹10 per case (per record scored) for compute hosted on our servers. Model building, validation, reports and Nu Score bands are included.
